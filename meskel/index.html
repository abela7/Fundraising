About the Feast

The Finding of the True Cross

Meskel commemorates the historic 4th-century finding of the True Cross of Christ by Saint Helena (Queen Eleni), who was guided to its hidden resting place by the fragrant smoke of sweet incense.

Celebrated as the Ethiopian New Year blooms, this ancient festival of light, peace, and renewal is inscribed by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Our Local Gathering

Celebrating in Liverpool

While traditional celebrations in Ethiopia feature large open bonfires, we have thoughtfully adapted our ceremony to be peaceful, reverent, and safe for our surrounding community.

Sacred Incense

Instead of heavy smoke or open flame, we burn fragrant Frankincense in sacred censers — directly honouring the heavenly incense smoke Saint Helena followed to locate the Cross.

The Cross & Demera

A Holy Cross stands elevated at the center of gathered branches and greenery — representing the mountain of earth that was dug through in faith to reveal the Cross.

Prayer & Joyful Songs

Clergy, choir, and neighbours gather outside in front of the church for communal prayer, Saint Yared’s liturgical chants, and joyful songs of praise.
