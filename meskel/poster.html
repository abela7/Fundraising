ABOUT THE CELEBRATION

The Ethiopian calendar is embarking into the New Year, 2019!

We commemorate the finding of the True Cross by St. Helena. This is one of UNESCO World Heritage of Ethiopian culture. Usually, it is an outdoor event (use the QR to see how it is celebrated in Ethiopia).

We would like to have just the feel for it, as the circumstances allow us to practise.

The adaptation is to use incense instead of flame and smoke, while gathering outside the church at St. Gabriel's Church for prayer and praise.

We will have a cross at the centre of the built-up bushes (to represent the mountain of rubbish that had to be dug out to get the Cross).

We will then sing joyful songs to celebrate the finding of the Cross.
